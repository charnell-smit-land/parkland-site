What a pattern looks like

Your Business Is Already Telling You What to Do Next.

Patterns are the things your business keeps doing, good and bad, whether anyone has noticed or not. Here are the kinds we find, and what we do about them.

Customers

Your best customers all found you the same way.

The movePut more behind the channel that brings your best customers, and stop spreading the budget evenly across everything.

Operations

Every busy stretch is followed by a wave of complaints.

The moveFix the handoff that breaks under pressure before you spend a dollar bringing in more volume.

Communication

The same question shows up in reviews, DMs and customer emails.

The moveAnswer it everywhere customers look, before they have to ask, and watch the friction disappear.

Loyalty

Customers who come back once tend to keep coming back.

The moveDesign the whole experience around earning that second visit, because that is where loyalty starts.

Common Questions

What Does Parkland Consulting Group Do?

Parkland Consulting Group is a pattern-led growth strategy firm based in Atlanta. We help small and growing businesses read their data, signals and patterns, then decide what to focus on next: where to grow, how to scale and how to communicate. Services include a fixed-fee Pattern Audit, Growth Strategy, Scale Strategy and Communication Strategy.

Who Founded Parkland?

Parkland was founded by Charnell Smith-Landry, a strategist with a 25-year career in digital, marketing and technology, including more than 15 years of independent consulting. She has worked with national companies like Cox Communications and Inspire Brands and with founder-led small businesses across many industries.

What Kinds of Businesses Do You Work With?

Small and growing organizations in any industry. Clients have included retail and ecommerce shops, beauty and wellness studios, restaurants, early education centers and service businesses. The industry changes the details, not the approach: we read your data and signals and show you what to focus on.

Do You Only Work With Businesses in Atlanta?

No. Parkland is based in Atlanta and works with businesses across the United States.
