Privacy Policy

Parkland Consulting Group respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and our services, and how we use it.

Last updated November 25, 2026

Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you book a call, contact us or become a client, we collect details like your name, email address, phone number, business name and anything you choose to share about your business.

Payment information. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card or bank account numbers. Stripe handles them under its own privacy policy.

Website usage. We may use privacy-friendly analytics that count visits and pages viewed without using advertising cookies or building a profile of you.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to schedule and hold calls, deliver our services, send invoices and receipts, respond to your questions and, if you sign up, send occasional updates you can unsubscribe from at any time. We do not sell your information.

Client Business Information

Information you share about your business during an engagement, such as reports, customer data or platform access, is used only to deliver our services and is kept confidential. We return or delete it when the engagement ends, unless you ask us to keep it or we are required to retain it.

Services We Use

We rely on trusted providers to run the business, including Cal.com for scheduling, Stripe for payments, Google Workspace for email and Cloudflare for hosting this website. Each handles information under its own privacy and security practices.

Your Choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing info@parkland.consulting.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy from time to time and will change the date above when we do. Questions? Email info@parkland.consulting.
