Refund Policy

Parkland Consulting Group provides professional services, not physical products, so there is nothing to return. Here's how refunds work for each type of engagement.

Last updated November 25, 2026

Pattern Audit

If you cancel before your kickoff call, you'll receive a full refund. Once the kickoff call has taken place, work on your audit has begun and payments are non-refundable. If you chose two payments, the second payment is due as invoiced.

Project Work

Refund terms for project work are set out in each engagement agreement, which you'll review and approve before any payment is due.

Advisory and Fractional Retainers

Retainers are billed monthly in advance and can be cancelled with written notice before the next billing date. Months already billed are non-refundable. Any other terms are set out in your engagement agreement.

How to Request a Refund

Email invoices@parkland.consulting with your name, business name and invoice number. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days.

Questions

If something isn't working, talk to us first. We would always rather fix it with you.
