What We Look At

Customers

How people find you, buy from you and come back, and where they drop off.

For exampleMost repeat customers make the same first purchase.

Operations

The workflows, handoffs and recurring friction your teams deal with every day.

For exampleOne handoff causes most of the complaints.

Performance

The numbers you already track, read for trends instead of single moments.

For exampleSales dip the same week every month.

Technology

Your digital tools and how well they talk to each other and support growth.

For exampleTwo tools collect the same data, and neither talks to the other.
