Common Questions

What Is a Pattern Audit?

A Pattern Audit is a fixed-fee, three-to-four-week review of your customers, operations, performance and technology. It finds the patterns behind your results, what keeps working and what keeps getting in the way, and turns them into a ranked, 90-day action plan you can act on right away.

How Is It Different From a Marketing Audit?

A marketing audit usually looks at channels on their own. A Pattern Audit looks at how your customers, team, operations and tools work together, because the real cause of a problem often sits outside marketing.

What Do You Need From Me?

A kickoff call, access to your key reports and tools, and time for the readout. We handle the analysis, so it fits around running your business.

What Do I Get at the End?

A readout session with your top patterns and recommended moves, a 90-day action plan, and a 30-day check-in after the readout. If you book project work within two weeks, you save 15%.

Can I Pay Over Time?

Yes. The investment starts from $3,000, or two payments of $1,500.
