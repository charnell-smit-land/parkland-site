Support

How Can We Help?

The quickest way to reach us is by email. We reply within 48 hours, Monday through Friday, not counting weekends or holidays.

Payments and Invoices

For questions about an invoice, a payment, a receipt or a refund, email invoices@parkland.consulting.

To help us respond quickly, include your name, your business name and your invoice number if you have one.

See Your Invoices Anytime

Use Client Login to view and pay invoices, download receipts and update your payment method. Sign in with the email address your invoices are sent to.

Everything Else

For questions about your engagement or our services, email info@parkland.consulting. Current clients can also reply directly to any email from us.

Prefer to talk? Call (678) 587-8299, Monday through Friday. If we miss you, leave a message and we'll call you back within 48 hours.

Business Hours and Holidays

We're available Monday through Friday. Parkland is closed during the last two weeks of December and reopens on the first business day of the new year. We're also closed on U.S. federal holidays: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day.

Messages sent while we're closed are answered within 48 hours of our return, Monday through Friday.
