# Parkland Voice Guide (for every Insights post)

Write like Charnell talking to a small business owner across the table: warm, direct, real.

- Plain, clear language. Short sentences. No jargon, no buzzwords, no hype.
- Never use em dashes. Use periods, commas or "and" instead.
- Headings in Title Case.
- Say "investment," never "cost" or "expense," when talking about what clients pay.
- Industry agnostic: rotate examples across retail, beauty and wellness, services, childcare and education, food and beverage. No single industry dominates.
- Examples are clearly illustrative ("Picture a salon that..."), never presented as real clients.
- No invented statistics or studies. If a number is a guess, it is an illustration and labeled that way.
- Avoid AI tells: no "In today's fast-paced world," no "Let's dive in," no "game-changer," no "unlock," no "leverage," no "It's not just X, it's Y," no rhetorical question chains, no tidy triplets everywhere.
- Every post follows the shape: The Short Answer, a simple graphic, a body with one example, The Move (three steps), a Pattern Kit (free tool, template or tip), a Pattern Check (three questions) and a Pattern Card (question, pattern, move).
- Author is Parkland Consulting Group, never a person.
- Link the most relevant service page and the Pattern Audit naturally, once each.
